==================================================

The name "Wijhan" | "وِجهان" is inspired by the Arabic concept of:

"وِجهة"

Meaning:

Direction.

Destination.

The path toward a goal.

The philosophy behind Wijhan is not simply:

"Where are we going?"

It is:

"Why are we going there in the first place?"

Before writing code, Wijhan wants to understand:

- Who is the real customer?

- What is the actual business problem?

- What does success look like?

- What should be built first?

- What should NOT be built?

- How should technology support the business?

Wijhan acts as a technology and product partner rather than simply taking requirements and executing them.
